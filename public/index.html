The platform

Everything money movement needs,
in one clean surface.

PayoutFlow replaces a patchwork of spreadsheets, bank portals and scripts with a single, auditable system of record for every cent that leaves your account.

Global payouts

Send to bank accounts, mobile money, cards and wallets across 120+ corridors, with local rails and transparent FX.

Real-time ledger

Every credit and debit is an immutable entry. Your balance is always derived from history, never guessed — so reconciliation takes seconds.

Hosted checkout

A conversion-tuned payment page that accepts cards and local methods. Card data goes straight to the processor, keeping you out of PCI scope.

Idempotent & safe

Retries never double-pay. Idempotency keys, atomic balance checks and signed webhooks make every operation safe to replay.

Developer first

REST API, webhooks with HMAC signatures, and a full audit trail. Ship an integration in an afternoon, not a quarter.

Instant settlement

Median settlement under two seconds on local rails, with live status events streaming back to your systems as they happen.

Three steps, start to finish

No account to create and no app to install. The whole flow runs in the browser.

1

Pick a method

Choose card, GCash, PayMaya, bank transfer, wallet or WU/MG and see the payment details straight away.

2

Send the payment

Pay with the details shown, then mark it as sent. Amounts and accounts stay on screen so nothing is mistyped.

3

Upload the receipt

Attach a screenshot of the payment. It is kept locally for review, so nothing is uploaded to a server.
